    Your first time, in six steps

    Most people finish in under five minutes on their phone.

    1. Sign in with GoogleOpen the link and tap Continue with Google. No Gmail? Use a username and password instead.
    2. Join your productionEnter your show’s code and the mobile number your Line Producer has. Your deal is already waiting.
    3. Add your photoA clear photo of your face. It goes on your Crew ID.
    4. Profile and bankYour name exactly as on your PAN card, your address, PAN and bank account. Payments go here.
    5. Aadhaar and PANPhotos of both. Cover the first 8 Aadhaar digits, then type the last 4.
    6. Sign your agreementYou get a notification when it’s ready. Read it, type your name and sign with your finger.

    How a show runs in CrewDesk

    Set up once, then every person moves through the same chain: invite, agreement, schedule, invoice, payment, Tally.

    1. Company details, onceThe CFO or Commercial Head enters PAN, TAN, GSTIN and bank. Every show uses them.
    2. Create the showIt gets its own joining code. Share it with the crew; make a new one if it leaks.
    3. Onboard peopleSet the deal and send the invite on WhatsApp. They fill their own bank, PAN and Aadhaar.
    4. AgreementsSend with one tap when someone is ready. Owners approve after they sign.
    5. Schedule and gatePlan shoot days and check people in with their Crew ID. That’s their attendance.
    6. Invoices to TallyHOD check, final approval, deductions, one payment run, then the Tally files.

    Who does what

    ★CFO and Commercial Head

    • Company details and new productions
    • Approve signed agreements and invoices
    • Change deals (every change can be undone)
    • Deductions, removals and reports

    ◆Line Producer and Head of Production

    • Onboard people and send agreements
    • Plan shoot days and check people in
    • HOD check on invoices
    • Ask the owners to remove someone

    ₹Accounts

    • Pay approved invoices in one bank run
    • Deductions and remarks before payment
    • TDS and GST registers, Tally files

    ●Crew, artists and vendors

    • Fill their own details and sign
    • Crew ID at the gate every shoot day
    • Raise invoices and follow them on the bell

    Common questions

    I forgot my password

    On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. If your account uses a Gmail address, tap Sign in with Google instead: you’re straight back in. Otherwise we email you a reset link.

    It says the production code is wrong

    Every show has its own code, like SATH-7Q4K. Check it with your Line Producer. After 10 wrong tries, joining is paused for an hour.

    My deal, rate or name isn’t showing

    Use the same mobile number or Gmail your Line Producer used for your invite. If it still doesn’t show, ask them to check your invite.

    Where is my agreement?

    It arrives after your profile and documents are complete. You’ll get a notification on the bell, and it appears under My setup → Agreement.

    Where is my invoice right now?

    Open My invoices: each one shows its stage (waiting for HOD, waiting for the CFO or Commercial Head, approved, paid). The bell tells you each time it moves.

    Who can see my PAN, Aadhaar and bank details?

    Only you, the CFO, the Commercial Head and Accounts on your production. Every change is logged.
