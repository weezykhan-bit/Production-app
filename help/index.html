Help · for crew, artists and vendors

Set up your CrewDesk account in 2 minutes

Watch the video, then open the link your Line Producer sent you. You need your production code, your PAN card, your bank details and a photo of your Aadhaar.

  • Works on any phone
  • Sign in with Google
  • Your details stay private

Your first time

  1. 1Sign in with GoogleOpen the link and tap Continue with Google. No Gmail? Use a username and password instead.
  2. 2Join your productionEnter the production code and the mobile number your Line Producer has. Your deal is already waiting for you.
  3. 3Add your photoA clear photo of your face. It goes on your Crew ID.
  4. 4Profile and bankYour name exactly as on your PAN card, your address, PAN and bank account. Payments go to this account.
  5. 5Aadhaar and PANPhotos of both. Cover the first 8 Aadhaar digits, then type the last 4.
  6. 6Sign your agreementYou get a notification when it’s ready. Read it, type your name and sign with your finger.

After that

On every shoot day

  • Show your Crew ID code at the gate. It’s at the top of My setup.
  • Your check-ins count as your working days.
  • See call times and locations in Shoot schedule.

Getting paid

  • Go to My invoices and tap Raise invoice.
  • Pick the dates. Your days fill in from the gate.
  • The bell tells you when it’s checked, approved and paid, with the bank reference.

Your privacy

  • Only you, the CFO, the Commercial Head and Accounts see your PAN, Aadhaar and bank details.
  • Every change to your deal is recorded and you’re told about it.

For the production office

Line Producer and Head of Production

  • Artists, crew & vendors → Onboard: set the deal and copy the WhatsApp invite.
  • Send agreements once someone’s details are complete.
  • Plan shoot days and check people in at the gate.
  • Check invoices, and ask the owners to remove someone if needed.

CFO and Commercial Head

  • Set up company details once.
  • Approve signed agreements and invoices.
  • Change deals (every change can be undone), add deductions and remove people.
  • Reports and Excel for each month.

Accounts

  • Payments & Tally: pay approved invoices in one bank run.
  • Deductions and remarks before payment.
  • TDS and GST registers, and the Tally files.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. If your account uses a Gmail address, tap Sign in with Google instead: you’re straight back in. Otherwise we email you a reset link.

It says the production code is wrong

Check it with your Line Producer; codes look like SATH-7Q4K. After 10 wrong tries, joining is paused for an hour.

My deal, rate or name isn’t showing

Use the same mobile number or Gmail your Line Producer used for your invite. If it still doesn’t show, ask them to check your invite.

Where is my agreement?

It arrives after your profile and documents are complete. You’ll get a notification on the bell, and it appears under My setup → Agreement.

Something else

Tap the Guide button in the app and ask, or contact your Line Producer.
